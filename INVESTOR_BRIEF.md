# PackProof — investment discussion brief

**Less virgin plastic. Evidence attached.**

A local decision workbench that turns packaging specifications into component-level comparisons, use checks and a supplier-ready next action. Current stage: working prototype with synthetic scenario verification and a bounded local-model test. No revenue, retailer commitment or measured customer savings is claimed.

## Why now

Packaging accounts for 40% of plastics used in the EU, according to the European Commission. Its packaging-waste page lists 12 August 2026 as the PPWR application date. Eurostat reports 35.3 kg of plastic packaging waste per EU resident and a 42.1% recycling rate in 2023. These are independently sourced context, not market size or product impact. See the primary links in EVIDENCE_LEDGER.md. Regulatory details require qualified review.

## The purchasing decision

A lighter package can contain more virgin plastic than a heavier package with high recycled content. But a low number is not sufficient: lid scope, food-contact conditions, product loss, line handling and costs can overturn a choice. Today’s alternative is a combination of spreadsheets, supplier PDFs, email and expert judgment. PackProof’s hypothesis is that keeping those records and decisions together reduces review effort and missed assumptions.

The proposed initial customer is a retail packaging/procurement team reviewing a specific category, with quality/food safety and operations as required reviewers. The first deliverable is a defensible shortlist and pilot brief, not an automated approval.

## Product and differentiation hypothesis

Deterministic arithmetic counts each component. Unknowns stay unknown or become bounded intervals. A local model proposes fields with page-associated source passages, while a person accepts, corrects or rejects them. Suitability remains separate from material reduction. A crossover calculation identifies the minimum recycled content that would change a ranking and turns it into a supplier question.

Local execution is a useful option for sensitive documents. It does not replace enterprise security. A future advantage could come from a validated component/source history embedded in recurring purchasing work. This is a hypothesis to test, not an established moat. No unsupported competitor comparisons are made.

## Commercial model to test

Begin with a fixed-scope paid review pilot, jointly agreed before work, then test an annual team subscription if repeat use is demonstrated. Pricing is not validated. Interview buyers about budget ownership, procurement cycles and their current cost of review before choosing a price. Separate software revenue from consulting or laboratory work.

Use a bottom-up opportunity model only after evidence exists: reachable retail accounts × relevant buying teams × validated annual willingness to pay. No addressable-market number is invented. Measure review hours saved, completeness of decision records, repeat-case use, supplier response time and conversion to qualified trials. Packaging cost and virgin reduction belong to the retailer’s separate, verified business case.

## Proposed six-week evidence plan

Weeks 1–2: obtain permission for 10 real specifications for one decision. Have a packaging reviewer establish reference fields and baseline review time. Log supported, ambiguous, conflicting and missing information; measure critical errors by field and document, with the sample size disclosed.

Weeks 3–4: shortlist 3 alternatives; obtain quotes, exact-SKU documentation and actual contact/line conditions. Proposed software success gates to agree with the partner: every accepted critical field has a reviewed supporting passage, zero unresolved critical mismatch at trial entry, and at least 30% less median review time on comparable tasks without more critical errors. These are proposed targets, not achieved results or general model accuracy.

Weeks 5–6: aim for 1 qualified retail trial after owners agree sample sizes and measurable acceptance limits. Test leakage, grease, product protection, shelf life, throughput, logistics and consumer handling/disposal. Stop on a critical failure. Calculate material and financial effects only from measured and sourced trial inputs.

## Expansion and risks

Expand from a successful category workflow to recurring reviews, then supplier revision tracking and portfolio decisions. Authenticated team access, role controls, robust audit logging, database storage and procurement integrations are roadmap items. The current app is local single-user software.

Principal risks: model misinterpretation despite a matching quote; weak supplier evidence; food-loss burden shifting; no demonstrated willingness to pay; expert review remaining the main bottleneck. Mitigations are human review, bounded claims, direct baseline measurement and early commercial discovery. No amount of visual polish removes the need for these results.

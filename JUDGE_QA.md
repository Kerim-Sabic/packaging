# Judge questions and live proof route

Answer directly, then show the relevant screen if asked. Do not read this page during the three-minute pitch.

## Why software instead of a new packaging material?

The challenge explicitly accepts digital decision support. PackProof helps the retailer compare existing options, identify evidence gaps and specify a qualified trial. It does not claim to have invented or certified a new material.

## Are the 12.4 tonnes real savings?

They are an illustrative scenario: (17 minus 4.6) grams times 1,000,000 eligible packs times 100% adoption, divided by 1,000,000 grams per tonne. Real impact needs measured masses, guaranteed content, actual volume and functional equivalence.

## Does this solve the 250°C hot-food requirement?

The example detects a mismatch; it does not solve it. We need the exact package specification, actual contact profile, food type, holding time and lid restrictions, followed by qualified trials.

## What if AI invents a number?

It proposes a field with a source passage and scope. The app checks schema and quotation presence, but semantic review remains human. No general accuracy claim follows from our small synthetic test. Manual entry and labelled replay remain available.

## What about shelf life, recyclability and consumer experience?

The pilot includes product loss, protection, grease, logistics, consumer handling/disposal and local collection evidence. Recycled content does not establish recyclability. Owners must agree measurable limits and stop on unacceptable trade-offs.

## Why would someone pay rather than use Excel?

Excel can do the arithmetic. Our commercial hypothesis is that a joined-up source review, use check and decision record saves review effort and missed assumptions. A paid scoped pilot tests that value before subscription pricing or broad rollout.

## How does it scale?

Start with one decision and category, then repeat where value is measured. Team access, permissions, stronger audit storage and procurement integrations are roadmap work. The delivered application is local and single-user.

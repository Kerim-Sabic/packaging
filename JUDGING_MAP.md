## Hot-food challenge coverage

The 250°C oven, 180–190°C rotisserie and up-to-six-hour holding values are challenge context, not proof of actual package contact conditions. Record the actual contact profile and whether the pack enters the oven or receives food after cooking. Case B intentionally exposes 250 versus 220 and 6 versus 2 mismatches. Do not claim a material solves the optional bag/box subchallenge without exact documentation and trials.

Nine pilot checks cover component content, intended use, sealing, line performance, grease/oil, shelf life/product loss, logistics/storage, consumer handling/disposal, and design-for-recycling/local collection. Use them to specify measurable limits with the retailer. Mono-material construction alone does not establish collection, sorting or actual recycling. Recycled content is not food-safety approval.

## Proposed rollout, subject to validation

**First 2 weeks:** review 10 permissioned real specifications from one purchasing decision; measure baseline review time, component completeness and critical extraction errors. Resolve every accepted claim against an exact passage.

**Weeks 3–6:** shortlist 3 alternatives; collect quotes and exact-SKU documentation. Agree the trial profile, sample size, acceptance thresholds and named owners. Run 1 trial only after critical evidence gaps and mismatches are resolved.

**After a qualified trial:** expand within the same category before adding another category. Add source revision comparison and a shared case register after workflow value is established.

**Later product work:** authenticated team access, roles, controlled storage, robust audit events, supplier-document versioning, portfolio analysis and procurement integrations. These are planned, not present capabilities.

The app now adds branded generated imagery and an explicit comparison-to-trial review path. This makes evidence gaps and next actions visible without awarding a fictional readiness or judging score.
